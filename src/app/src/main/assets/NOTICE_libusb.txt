libusb 1.0
============

IRPanoView ships libusb (LGPL-2.1-or-later) for native USB transport on the Hik
protocol path. Vendor libusb1.0.so extracted from TopInfrared reference APK for
arm64-v8a parity testing, or built from https://github.com/libusb/libusb.

See libusb/COPYING in the upstream source distribution.
